## Plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | {catalog.currency} 0 | One person trying receipt capture |
| **Professional** | {catalog.currency} {catalog.professionalPrice} per user per month | Teams that submit, approve and export expenses |
| **Enterprise** | Contact sales | Larger organizations that need a contract, invoiced payment terms, SSO or custom limits |
| **Portfolio** | Contact sales | Property managers: Professional's limits plus up to {catalog.portfolioPortalLogins} vendor portal logins |

Free and Professional can be chosen in the app under **Settings > Billing > Plans**. Enterprise and Portfolio are set up by the Smartbills sales team: contact [sales@smartbills.io](mailto:sales@smartbills.io).

<Note>
  Professional comes with a **{catalog.trialDays}-day free trial**, which starts when you choose it. The Free plan needs no credit card.
</Note>

The app shows prices in your workspace's billing currency.

## What each plan includes

| | Free | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receipt scans | {catalog.limits.free.receiptsPerMonth} a month (at most {catalog.limits.free.receiptsPerDay} a day) | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Manual expenses | {catalog.limits.free.manualEntriesPerMonth} a month | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Digital receipts from merchants | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Expense reports | {catalog.limits.free.expenseReportsPerMonth} a month | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Team members | Just you | Up to {catalog.limits.professional.invites} invited members | Unlimited |
| Bank connections | None | {catalog.limits.professional.bankConnectionsIncluded}, plus 1 per user | Unlimited |
| Connected mailboxes | None | {catalog.limits.professional.mailboxes} | Unlimited |
| Storage | {catalog.limits.free.storageMb} MB | {catalog.limits.professional.storageGbPerUser} GB per user | Unlimited |
| Categories and departments | {catalog.limits.free.categories} categories, {catalog.limits.free.departments} department | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Vault folders | {catalog.limits.free.folders} | {catalog.limits.professional.folders} | Unlimited |
| AI credits | {catalog.limits.free.aiCreditsPerMonth} a month | {catalog.limits.professional.aiCreditsPerUserPerMonth} per user per month | {catalog.limits.enterprise.aiCreditsPerUserPerMonth} per user per month |
| Audit log and delegation | No | Yes | Yes |
| Integrations | No | Yes | Yes |

Per-user allowances are pooled: a workspace with five users on Professional shares 5 GB of storage and 5 × {catalog.limits.professional.aiCreditsPerUserPerMonth} AI credits a month. Customer invoicing (accounts receivable) and the cash-application tools are add-ons on top of a plan.

When a limit is reached, the action that needs it is refused until the period resets or the plan changes. **Settings > Billing** shows your usage against each limit.

## Features that need a paid plan

Features gated by plan need **Professional or higher**. That covers the [audit log](/guides/web-app/settings/audit-log), [delegation](/guides/web-app/settings/delegation) and the [integrations](/integrations/overview) (accounting, banking, storage and messaging apps). Where a page says a feature needs "Professional or higher", any paid plan listed above qualifies.

## Enterprise contracts

An Enterprise workspace is billed under a contract rather than by card:

- A fixed term, renewed automatically unless you give notice by the date in the contract
- Payment by card, or by invoice with payment terms and an optional purchase order number
- Committed seats, scheduled price or seat changes, and one-time set-up or integration fees
- SSO, custom limits and a dedicated account manager

Changes to a contract go through your account manager.

## Legacy plans

Startup, Business and Scale are no longer sold. A workspace on one of them keeps its plan and limits until it changes plan; once it moves, it cannot go back.

<Note>
  The app may still label Professional as **Business**. They are the same plan.
</Note>
